    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, no mission replay, no New Game Plus — so the order matters. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission: it is your only way back. Work the five stages in order; the tracker above ticks off as you go.

    The one missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy launch coverage flags as possibly missable: one meditation spot — Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1 — is reported to disappear after that chapter. Meditate there during Chapter 1, before the chapter ends. Every other trophy is earned in free roam or by finishing missions, so nothing else in the list is reported missable at launch — but with no mission replay, the chapter-start saves are still your safety net.
