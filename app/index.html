Roadmap to the platinum

Play in this order. Stages 1 and 2 are the story and the people of Leonida; stage 3 is the long grind; stage 4 is the finish line. Only three trophies can be missed — all three are flagged below and in the checklist.

How missables are decided

A trophy is flagged missable only where the official description itself names a condition that can be passed or a choice that can lock it. That gives exactly three: Actions Speak Louder (choices can lock other paths), Bad Luck, I Guess (it needs a failed first attempt — succeeding first try may skip it), and The Truth Is Out There (a one-off strange event). Everything else is repeatable in the open world or earned by story progress, and story trophies are never flagged missable.
